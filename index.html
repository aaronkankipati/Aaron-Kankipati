V1 → V2 · Diagnose

I used my own product.

Here's what I found — not from a code review, but from opening the app like a real user.

Trust failure

Every poster showed the wrong film.

The wrong image on every recommendation card made every pick feel unreliable before a single word was read.

Core promise broken

Same films for every mood.

Moodfilm's entire premise is emotional relevance. If every mood returns the same blockbusters, the product simply doesn't work.

Backlog item #1

No Hindi or Telugu support.

Language selection didn't exist in V1. It became the most requested feature after launch — users told me what to build next.

V2 · The Product Decision

I hit a ceiling.

So I redesigned around it.

Adding OMDb alongside TMDb gave richer data — but two APIs pushed 170 calls per session against a free-tier limit of 50. The easy move was to upgrade the plan. Instead: understand the constraint, scope the solution, keep both sources. I batched and parallelised calls across both APIs and brought it down to 22 calls max. Stayed free.
