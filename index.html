Neon Bull

I gave an AI ₹10,000 and full control to trade the Indian stock market.

Neon Bull exists because of Project Zeon. I was building an AI trading system connected to a live brokerage account with real money. The APIs were integrated, the logic was in place, and everything was ready to go. But before handing an AI full control over real capital, I needed to know if the engine actually worked. So I built this first. Same system. Same logic. Same market. The only difference is the money is not real yet.

A controlled version of a bigger idea Neon Bull is Project Zeon running on paper capital. I seeded it with ₹10,000 of test money and let Claude trade the Indian market autonomously. The stocks are real. The prices are real. The brokerage charges are real. The execution logic is the same one I plan to use when real money is involved. This is the proving ground before that happens.
How it runs Two Cloudflare cron jobs fire every weekday at 9:30 AM IST and 1:30 PM IST. The Worker pulls live prices from Yahoo Finance NSE, feeds them to Claude Sonnet with RSI, SMA and volume data, and executes the trade. NSE holidays are auto-detected and skipped. No server. No manual trigger. It just runs.
Built with real guardrails Groww brokerage charges are simulated on every trade including STT, DP charges and GST, so the P&L reflects what real trading would cost. The experiment auto-pauses if the portfolio drops below starting capital. Stock names are fully hidden from the public dashboard to stay clear of any SEBI compliance concerns.
What feeds back into Project Zeon Every version of Neon Bull teaches me something. V1 trades twice a day. V2 will move to four sessions and a refined signal model. The learnings around profitability, brokerage costs, and signal quality all feed directly into how Project Zeon will work when it eventually runs on real capital. The market is winning right now. That is the point.

Project Zeon

Real money. Real trades. No human intervention once it's running.

Over the past few weekends I have been quietly building an AI-driven trading system connected to a live brokerage account. The API keys are in place. The systems are integrated. A lot of the groundwork is already done. But I stopped before going live, because giving an AI full access to a real account on day one felt like a decision that deserved more than enthusiasm. So I built Neon Bull first.

Why I paused Giving an AI complete control over a live brokerage account on day one is interesting. It is also risky. I had the keys, the integrations, and the technical setup ready to go. But I wanted to prove the engine works before it touches real money. That pause was a product decision, not a fear of shipping.
Neon Bull as the proving ground Neon Bull is Project Zeon running in a controlled environment. The stocks are real. The market is real. The prices, execution logic, and brokerage charges are all real. The only thing that is not real is the starting capital. I seeded it with ten thousand rupees of test money and let it run. Same system, lower stakes, real feedback.
What I am building toward Neon Bull V1 trades twice a day. V2 will move to four sessions daily. I am refining the engine to optimise more for profitability, account for brokerage costs more conservatively, and improve signal quality over trade frequency. Each version builds on the last. When the system shows it can consistently make better decisions in the real market, that is when Project Zeon goes live.
The honest goal Not to get rich quickly. The goal is to build something that survives real-world market conditions and keeps getting better. The market is winning right now and that is probably the most useful data I could have. No unrealistic backtests. No hype. Just curiosity, iteration, and learning in public.

Moodfilm

I am not a developer by profession. I am a Product guy. I built this anyway.

An AI film curator that starts with a simple question: how are you feeling right now? Tell it your mood, pick a language (English, Hindi, or Telugu) and it finds a film matched to that emotional moment, not your watch history. Product thinking over code.

Discover the need Most film apps recommend based on what you watched last week. I wanted something that started with how you feel right now. That gap felt worth solving.
Diagnose what was broken in v1 I used my own product the way a real user would. Every poster showed the wrong film. The same blockbusters came up for every single mood. And there was no Hindi or Telugu support, which was the most requested thing after launch. Three clear problems, three backlog items.
Prioritise and ship v2 Adding OMDb alongside TMDb pushed API calls to 170 per session against a free tier limit of 50. Rather than upgrade the plan, I redesigned the flow down to 22 calls, batched and parallelised. Claude now returns a numeric film ID so the poster is fetched directly. No guessing. Wrong poster eliminated. Language selection built in across every step.
The result Loading takes 30 to 60 seconds, so I filled that time with 20 rotating film facts to keep users from dropping off. Every card now shows streaming availability by region so users know exactly where to watch. One tap sharing built in natively. 166 live sessions, 0 errors, 94% of issues resolved. Total cost: $0.
